# iWeb v2.3

## New
- **Gold & Silver card:**
  - retail or spot price;
  - 24K, 22K and 18K per gram, plus sovereign;
  - silver per kg.
- **Markets card:** NIFTY and SENSEX at a glance; it opens the new Markets app.
- **Markets app:**
  - a watchlist with search and reordering;
  - interactive charts from 1 day to 5 years;
  - key stats and market status.
- **Spotlight:** instant answers for indices, stocks, gold and silver.

## Fixes
- Closing prices are picked up after the market closes.
- Typing amounts in the currency card works properly.
- Offline and server errors are labelled differently.
- Esc collapses expanded cards.
