# iWeb v2.7

## New
- **Glass clock:** the clock's digits are frosted glass over your wallpaper, as on the iOS 26 Lock Screen. It works with every clock font, including uploaded ones.
- **Choose the clock style:** Glass (the default) or Vibrant Colour, which uses your wallpaper's most vibrant colour. Pick it in Settings → Appearance → Clock or from the clock's right-click menu.
- **Quote widget:** the quote under the clock is now its own widget, so you can move it, resize it or remove it.

## Improvements
- The clock style is saved and included in backups.
- Existing layouts are split into the clock and the quote without moving either.
