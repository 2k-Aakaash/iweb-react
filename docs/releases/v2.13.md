# iWeb v2.13: Light and Dark, accent colours, Apps, Notification Center, Dock menus and movable windows

## New
- **Light, Dark and Auto:** choose iWeb's appearance in Settings → Appearance, as in macOS 26. Auto follows your system and switches with it.
- **Light appearance:** frosted glass you can see the wallpaper through, with dark text, across:
  - windows: Settings, Notes, Markets and News;
  - menus and right-click menus;
  - Control Center;
  - Spotlight;
  - dialogs.
- **Accent colour:** pick Multicolor, Blue, Purple, Pink, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green or Graphite in Settings → Appearance. Buttons, selections, switches, sliders, checkboxes, links, focus rings and Control Center follow it, in macOS's shade for each appearance.
- **Movable, resizable windows:** as in macOS.
  - Drag a window by its title area; resize from any edge or corner.
  - Drop it on the left or right edge of the screen to fill that half, or on the top to fill the screen; a frosted outline shows where it will land.
  - Double-click the title area to zoom.
  - The Window menu has Left Half, Right Half and Fill.
  - Each app remembers where its window was and how big.
- **Apps:** macOS 26's Apps view, in place of Launchpad.
  - Every app (Notes, Music, Markets, News, Spotlight, Wallpapers, Widgets, Settings) and every bookmark, in a full-screen grid over the blurred desktop.
  - Search as you type; All / Apps / Bookmarks; arrow keys and Enter.
  - Open it from the Dock's Apps icon, F4, or the iWeb menu → Apps.
  - Add App is now the + tile in Apps, and in the Apps icon's right-click menu.
- **Notification Center:** click the clock in the menu bar, as in macOS 26.
  - Notifications are grouped by app, with Clear and Clear All.
  - Below them: a Calendar widget, today's and overdue tasks from Notes, and a running timer.
  - New notifications show as a banner for a few seconds (not while Focus is on).
  - Notifications come from: the timer finishing, an iWeb update being available, tasks due today, and bookmarks removed from the Dock (click to undo).
- **Dock menus:** right-click anything in the Dock.
  - A bookmark: Open, Open in New Window, Copy Link, Edit in Settings…, Move Left / Right, Remove from Dock.
  - Notes: New Note and New Task. Music: Play / Pause, Next and Previous. Settings: quick links to its panes. Every app: Open / Show, and Quit while it runs.
  - The divider or the Dock itself: magnification on or off, Small / Medium / Large, and Dock Settings….
  - In edit mode, drag a bookmark up out of the Dock to remove it.
- **Bookmarks in Settings:** Settings → Bookmarks is now a macOS-style list of your Dock bookmarks.
  - Add with +, remove with − (with Undo).
  - Double-click to rename or change an address.
  - Drag, or press Alt+↑/↓, to reorder.
  - Changes show in the Dock straight away.
- **macOS alerts:** every browser pop-up is now a macOS 26 alert: the app icon, a bold title, a short message and capsule buttons (Enter confirms, Escape cancels). Destructive choices (delete a note, clear completed tasks, delete a playlist, clear the music library or search history) have a red button. Confirmations such as "Added to playlist" are notifications instead.
- **Add App:** a centred macOS sheet. Mistakes show under the fields instead of in a pop-up.
- **Removing a Dock icon** (the × in edit mode) works like Remove from Dock: it's gone at once, with Undo in its notification.

## Light, done the macOS way
- **Raised controls are white:** buttons, calculator keys, chips, tabs and the selected segment of segmented controls. Tracks, fields and wells stay a darker tint.
- **Readable colours:** coloured text and icons take deeper shades of their own colour (dark gold for Notes' yellow, green and red for Markets), and secondary text is solid enough to read over any wallpaper.
- **Visible controls:** switches have a clear off track, and sliders a visible track behind the accent fill.
- **Accent blue when on:** switched-on controls turn the accent blue.

## Improvements
- **Instagram Analytics removed:** it was listed in the widget gallery as "In Development" and is gone, along with its server code. Creator Widgets is YouTube Analytics.
- What sits on the wallpaper (the clock, menu bar, Dock, widgets and sticky cards) keeps its look in both appearances, as in macOS.
- Music keeps its own dark design in Light.
- Appearance and accent colour are saved and included in backups. Dark and Multicolor remain the defaults.
