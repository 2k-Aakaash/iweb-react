# iWeb v2.8: Control Center

## New
**Control Center**, as in macOS 26, opens from the menu bar.
- **Connectivity and media:** Wi-Fi status and details, and Now Playing with playback controls.
- **Display:** Focus, Display brightness and Night Shift.
- **Sound:** volume, mute and output device.
- **Widgets and clock:**
  - show or hide widgets;
  - switch the clock between Glass and Vibrant Colour;
  - Show Desktop and Edit Widgets.
- **Tools:**
  - a calculator, Screenshot and Full Screen;
  - a timer whose countdown shows beside the Dynamic Island and chimes in every iWeb tab.
- **Edit Controls:** add, remove and reorder controls.

## Improvements
- Control Center settings are saved and included in backups.
