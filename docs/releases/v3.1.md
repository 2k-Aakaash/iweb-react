# iWeb v3.1: Frontier World

## New
- **Frontier World:** an original frontier landscape to explore by ear, in its own window. It's generated live in your browser: no recordings, nothing downloaded.
  - **Five destinations, 25 places:**
    - **Goldgrass Plains:** prairie wind and meadowlarks at Golden Mile.
    - **Pinewater Valley:** a cold river at Pineglass River.
    - **Ironpeak Range:** a frozen tarn that sings at night at Glasswater Tarn.
    - **Cypress Lowlands:** frogs in the dark at Darkwater Fen.
    - **Sunscour Desert:** dust in the wind at Drywell Outpost.
  - **Time of day:** choose Dawn, Morning, Afternoon, Dusk or Night. Or let it follow your clock, or speed the day up so it passes in 1–12 hours. The birds' dawn chorus builds and the crickets join through dusk.
  - **Weather:** Clear, Partly Cloudy, Overcast, Light Rain, Heavy Rain, Thunderstorm and Fog, with Snowfall in the mountains and Dust Wind in the desert. Choose one, or let it change by itself, slowly, as each destination's climate allows. Rain arrives as a front: the wind rises and thunder rolls in the distance first. Fog muffles distant sounds.
  - **Life:** now and then, a hawk overhead, coyotes or a wolf at night (sometimes answered from further off), cattle, a train crossing the plain, a steamboat whistle, a bell across the water. Never two at once.
  - **Three experiences:**
    - **Environmental:** the place itself.
    - **Cinematic:** original music kept quietly under it.
    - **Frontier Radio:** music first, with generated songs and the place softly behind them.
  - **Your mix:** volume and mute for every layer, an ambience volume, a music volume for each mode, and sliders for how often things happen and how strong the weather is.
  - **The window:** destinations and places on the left, the place's landscape in the middle (it follows the hour and the weather), every control on the right, and Now Playing along the bottom. On a narrow window the side panels slide in when you need them.
- **Where to find it:** Apps (search "frontier"), its Dock icon while it's open, Atmosphere's Frontier tab and Explore, or the Dynamic Island. The island shows the place, the weather and the experience, and its chip switches between Ambience, Cinematic and Radio.

## Improvements
- **Mute any layer** in Atmosphere without losing its level.
- **Scenes** saved at a Frontier place keep its time and weather.
- **Recovering from an environment that can't start:** the next one you choose now plays, instead of staying silent.
