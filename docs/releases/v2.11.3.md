# iWeb v2.11.3

## Fixes
- **Smart Stack:** scrolling always flips between cards. At medium size, the hourly forecast had made most of the card unresponsive.
- **Markets search:** international listings such as NVIDIA and Apple are now found, after NSE/BSE listings. Prices show in the listing's own currency.
- **Currency:** when the primary rate source is unreachable, today's ECB rates are used before falling back to cached rates, so conversions stay accurate.
