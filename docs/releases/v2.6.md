# iWeb v2.6

## New
- **News:** a News widget (small, medium or large) with rotating headlines, and a News app with sections, search, a story view and offline reading.
- **Desktop widgets:**
  - **Edit Widgets** lets you drag, resize and remove any widget;
  - widgets snap into a macOS 26-style grid;
  - Weather, Currency, Gold & Silver and Markets can leave the Smart Stack and sit on the desktop.
- **Widget gallery:** a macOS-style gallery in Settings → Widgets, with search and size previews. Click a widget or drag it out to add it.
- **Menu bar:**
  - a macOS 26 menu bar showing the front app's menus, Spotlight, Wi-Fi, battery and the clock;
  - optional auto-hide;
  - a zoomed window's traffic lights move into the menu bar.
- **Right-click menu:** macOS-style on the desktop.

## Improvements
- An expanded widget comes to the front and dims the rest of the desktop.
- The search bar can be removed. Ctrl K still opens Spotlight.
- The Markets window can be minimised and zoomed, and stock search finds common company names.
