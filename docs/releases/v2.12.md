# iWeb v2.12: Dock size and magnification

## New
- **Dock size:** choose any size from small to large in Settings → Appearance → Dock, or drag the Dock's divider up or down, as in macOS 26.
- **Magnification:** Off, Small, Medium or Large. The hover wave grows in proportion to the Dock's size.
- **Shrink to fit:** add as many apps as you like. When they don't fit the window, every icon shrinks evenly so they all stay visible, even while magnified, and grows back when there's room.
- **macOS 26 controls:**
  - sliders have a thin accent-filled track and a capsule knob that turns into a glass lens while dragged;
  - checkboxes and radio buttons match macOS 26;
  - Music's settings use macOS switches and pop-up menus.

## Improvements
- The Dock's padding, corners, divider and running indicators scale with its size.
- Dock settings are saved and included in backups.
