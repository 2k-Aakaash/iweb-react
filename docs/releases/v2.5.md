# iWeb v2.5: the macOS 26 redesign

## New
- **Liquid Glass:** glass surfaces across the Dock, labels and windows. Choose Static or Liquid mode in Settings → Appearance.
- **Windows:**
  - a genie animation when minimising to the Dock;
  - working minimise and zoom, and macOS 26 traffic lights;
  - running indicators in the Dock.
- **Notes:** Markdown with a Preview mode, undo and redo, and a macOS 26 sidebar.
- **Spotlight:** the macOS 26 design, with an inline top hit and category chips.
- **Settings:**
  - the macOS 26 layout, with a floating sidebar, back and forward, and grouped cards;
  - About and Data Controls pages.

## Improvements
- macOS 26 icons for the built-in apps.
- Website favicons are cached and refreshed weekly.
- Wallpapers can be downloaded, and Unsplash photos load faster.
- Your profile photo is stored locally in IndexedDB.
