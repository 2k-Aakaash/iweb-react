# iWeb v2.11.4

## Improvements
- **Control Center:**
  - the desktop behind it is softly blurred and dimmed, as in macOS 26;
  - it animates out when closed;
  - tiles no longer wiggle in Edit Controls, and the remove buttons are no longer cut off.
- **Menu bar:** when auto-hidden, it appears as soon as the pointer nears the top of the page or moves past it into the browser's tab strip. Brushing past the top doesn't trigger it.
- **Widget cards:** Weather, Currency and Gold & Silver expand and collapse in one smooth motion and land exactly on their final size, with no jump or cut-off.
