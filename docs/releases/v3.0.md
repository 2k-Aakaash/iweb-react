# iWeb v3.0: Atmosphere

## New
- **Atmosphere:** living ambient soundscapes, generated in your browser. There are no recordings and nothing is downloaded.
  - **Six environments:** Rainy Mood, Deep Space, Forest, Ocean, Fireplace and Dreamscape.
  - **Living Atmosphere:** the sound slowly evolves while it plays, choosing from Off, Gentle, Dynamic and Organic. Your mix and volume always stay as you set them.
  - **Scenes:** one click for a tuned sound. There are seven built in: Deep Focus, Midnight Coding, Rainy Reading, Forest Escape, Ocean Meditation, Late Night and Fireside. You can save your own, rename them, delete them and add favourites.
  - **Stereo:** Width and Movement place the layers around you and let them drift. It's best with headphones.
  - **Mix:** each layer, plus Mood, Variation and Depth.
- **In the Dynamic Island:** the scene, live level bars from the actual sound, volume, play and pause, shuffle, the next environment, and the Evolution mode. Each environment has its own subtle colour.
- **Focus sessions:** 25, 45, 60 or 90 minutes, or your own length. They show the time left, fade out gently at the end, and then stop or pause.
- **Edge glow:** a soft light along the edges of the screen in the environment's colours. It can follow the sound, or be turned off.
- **Suggestions:**
  - When you change the wallpaper, Atmosphere can suggest a scene to match, and you can link a scene to a wallpaper yourself.
  - Optionally, it suggests a scene for the time of day.
  - Suggestions never play by themselves.
- **Where to find it:** Apps, the Dynamic Island, or its Dock icon while it's open. The window has three tabs: Explore, Customize and Experience.

## Improvements
- **Island level bars:** animated without re-laying out the page, which makes music playback lighter too.
- **Lightweight:** no audio is created until you press Play. Only one iWeb tab plays at a time, and it uses almost nothing when paused. Settings and scenes are saved and included in backups.
