# iWeb v3.2: Faster, clearer glass

## New
- **Genie effect switch:** Settings → General → Dock. Turn it off and apps simply scale to and from their Dock icon, which is much lighter on slower computers.
- **Windows show what's really behind them:** Settings, Notes, Music and the other windows now blur the Dock, widgets and other windows behind them, not only the wallpaper.

## Improvements
- **Glass that keeps up:** the glass no longer flickers or "refreshes" when the Dock magnifies, or when you move windows and widgets.
- **Glass ready at start:** the desktop appears with its glass already drawn.
- **No glass gap after opening an app:** the glass stays in place as the genie ends.
- **Faster start in Focus:** widgets hidden by Focus aren't loaded until you need them.
- **Your clock font from the first frame:** a custom or chosen font no longer flashes the default font first.
- **Search bar and Dock corners** are right from the start.
- **One glass for everyone:** the Static/Liquid choice is gone. Everyone gets the smooth static glass, and the heavier Liquid renderer has been removed.

## Fixes
- **Music's window** now has its frosted glass.
- **Windows are no longer too dark** over dark wallpapers.
- **Light appearance:** lowering the display brightness in Control Center no longer looks inverted.
